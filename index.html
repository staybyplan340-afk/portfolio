About Me

A curious learner, problem solver, and aspiring software developer building my journey one step at a time.

I'm Alhassan A. Hakim, an aspiring software developer from Bimbilla, Ghana. I recently completed my studies at Tamale Senior High School, where my interest in technology, problem-solving, and computer science continued to grow.

My journey into web development started with curiosity and a desire to learn by doing. I believe that even difficult experiences can teach us valuable lessons, and that mindset motivates me to keep learning, building, and improving one step at a time.

I'm currently focused on developing my technical skills and creating digital experiences that are simple, useful, and meaningful. This portfolio is one of the first steps in that journey.
